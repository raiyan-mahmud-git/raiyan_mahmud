A complete chess engine, interactive board and analysis suite with zero dependencies, running entirely in the browser. It is built on a 0x88 board representation, tapered evaluation and alpha-beta negamax search, and reads and writes FEN and PGN.

Flo, a personal finance tracker

Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, PWA

A modular finance tracker with no backend, accounts or tracking. You track transactions, manage budgets and savings goals, visualize your spending, and keep working offline thanks to PWA support.

Privacy note. Data stays in your browser's localStorage on your own device. It is not encrypted and is lost if you clear site data, so this is a learning project, not a place for sensitive financial details.

Takeaway. Managing app state and persistence entirely in the browser with the LocalStorage API, with no backend.

SMS spam classifier, two ways

Python, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras, FastAPI, Streamlit

The same problem solved twice. A TF-IDF and Naive Bayes model with cross-validated tuning scores 98.6% accuracy on a held-out test set of 1,115 messages. A Bidirectional LSTM built with TensorFlow and Keras scores 98.84% accuracy and 0.955 F1 on the spam class, on an internal holdout of 774 messages. It is served through a Streamlit demo and an API-key-protected FastAPI endpoint.

These are development results on two different splits, not a head-to-head comparison or an independent real-world evaluation.
